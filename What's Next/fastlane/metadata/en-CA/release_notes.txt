What's Next? has been rebuilt for iOS 27.

• iCloud sync: your recommendations now stay up to date across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
• A fresh design throughout the app.
• New Up Next widget that shows a random pick for you to try next, plus refreshed widgets. Tapping a widget opens the app right where you need it.
• Add a recommendation from Control Center, the Lock Screen, or the Action button.
• New Shortcuts and Siri actions: get what's next, open a recommendation, add a recommendation, and delete recommendations.
• Find your recommendations in Spotlight search.
• Support for iPhone Duo's folded and unfolded displays.
• Fixes: editing a recommendation no longer flags a change for stray spaces, and future recommendation dates are no longer allowed.
